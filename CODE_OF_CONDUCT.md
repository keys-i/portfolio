# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Contributors, maintainers, and project leaders commit to a community free from harassment. Everyone
is welcome regardless of age, body or disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
or expression, experience, education, economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste,
colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We will act in ways that keep Rad’s Portfolio open, inclusive, healthy, and welcoming.

## Our standards

Positive participation includes:

- showing empathy and kindness;
- respecting different viewpoints and experiences;
- offering and receiving useful feedback with care;
- owning mistakes, apologising, and learning from them; and
- putting the health of the wider community ahead of individual advantage.

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- sexualised language, imagery, attention, or advances;
- insults, trolling, derogatory remarks, or personal and political attacks;
- harassment in public or private;
- sharing someone’s private information without clear permission; and
- conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional community.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project leaders interpret and enforce these standards. They may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, reviews, and other contributions that violate this policy. When appropriate,
they will explain moderation decisions and take fair action against threatening, offensive, or
harmful behaviour.

## Scope

This policy applies in every project space and whenever someone officially represents the project,
including through an official account or at an online or in-person event.

## Reporting

Report abuse or harassment privately through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/keys-i/portfolio/security/advisories/new) and begin the
report with “Conduct report.” Do not open a public issue. Project leaders will review reports
promptly and fairly and will protect each reporter’s privacy and safety as far as possible.

## Enforcement guidelines

Project leaders use the following community-impact ladder:

### 1. Correction

For isolated unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour, leaders may give a private explanation and
written correction. They may also ask for a public apology.

### 2. Warning

For a clear violation or repeated conduct, leaders may issue a warning and prohibit contact with
the people involved for a defined period. Breaking those conditions can lead to a temporary or
permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary ban

For serious or sustained misconduct, leaders may temporarily prohibit all project interaction and
public communication. Contact with the people involved is forbidden during the ban.

### 4. Permanent ban

For a persistent pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, or disparagement of groups,
leaders may permanently remove the participant from public project interaction.
